SCENE 15: What I know, I know from books, and books lie

ME
(this is less insufferable to read if you do it in a voice)
What I know, I know from books, and books lie.

I know very little, about very few things. But the things I do know, might interest you. No, I am not on my fourth cup of coffee. I only drink Chicorée.

Why am I here? Why are you here, reading this now? I don't know, but if you do, please share.

The truth is I don't particularly long for the halcyon days, if there ever was, of the internet or computing. Sure, there are the memories of playing Pinball for 8 hours straight on the family computer, periodically interrupted by the neighborhood trouble maker inviting me for a round of WWE on the console, or trying to woohoo in the sims 2 with my parents in the room without getting caught. And these memories, paltry as they are, are my treasures.

Those were my good old days. What I mean when I say "kids these days don't get it." It's the sims on a computer with a higher virus count than frames per second.

What was I talking about? Ah yes, I'm bad at web design and this is my excuse for why my site looks like it's straight out of the rejected category for the awwwards.
